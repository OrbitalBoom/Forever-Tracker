Character

Your name, class, and level drive the upgrade finder, talent points, leveling route, and goal suggestions. Changes are saved automatically. You can change any of this later.

Goals

Track Classic Era dungeons (Ragefire Chasm through Scholomance and Stratholme), raids through Naxxramas, notable mounts, and milestone “achievements.” Add your own for Forever-specific content.

Gold

Log gold in g / s / c (100 copper = 1 silver, 100 silver = 1 gold). The running total and chart update as you add entries.

Farms

Best gold farms for your level, valued at today's auction prices. The guide uses your level and, when you have them, the professions on the Professions tab.

AH flip finder

Items listed under their usual price on the US Normal Horde auction house, so you can buy and resell them. No estimates are invented — if a sale rate or deposit is unknown, it is left blank.

Professions

The guide below follows those skills: it starts at where you are, marks earlier work as done, and shows the next crafts and materials (with auction prices when we have them). Changing skill in the guide updates the numbers here. Profession caps are 300 in Classic Era.

Items

Click a slot on the paper doll to see upgrades at your level. Use “Set what I’m wearing” in the slot panel to find any item for that slot — it ignores filters and includes Classic-reference items — then pin it as equipped. Items that can roll a random suffix (of the Monkey, of the Owl, and so on) list those options; pick one when you equip, and upgrade lists score each piece by its best suffix for the selected spec. You can also filter upgrades by type or minimum rarity, and sort by spec, rarity, item level, or required level. Search the item database below for uses, sources, and auction prices.

Item database

Search any item for its uses, where it drops or is sold, and live auction prices. Gear that is an upgrade for your character is marked so you can equip it.

Leveling Route

Hand-picked 1–60 routes for every race and both factions, from the public RestedXP Forever guides (free through ~21–22) and OpenXP (through 60). Choose a source and a Speedrun or Dungeon variant. Checkboxes, notes, and skip are saved automatically, per character.

Talents

WoW: Forever talent calculator (reworked trees, client 1.60.1). Your first point is at level 10 and you have 51 points at 60. Milestone 1-point talents sit at 11, 16, 21, and 31 points in a tree; there is no 41-point talent. Each row still needs 5 points in that tree, and some talents have prerequisites. Left-click to add a point, right-click to remove. Dual Specialization unlocks at level 40. Load a published, community, or community-consensus build to step through every level from 10 to 60 and see what comes next — that preview never changes your character’s saved level. A community heat map on the trees shows how often each talent is taken in the most popular community builds for a spec. A Classic Era (reference) toggle keeps the old 1.12 trees for comparison.
